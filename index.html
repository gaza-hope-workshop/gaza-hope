Inside freezing, damp tents across Gaza, newly amputated children lie helplessly on bare dirt. Their open surgical wounds face constant threat of severe, fatal infection. We refuse to stand by and watch them bleed on the earth. Born from the ashes, Gaza Hope Workshop digs through shattered concrete with bare hands to extract buried steel rebar. We hammer, bend, and hand-weld each bar into sturdy, elevated medical beds. Every single bed we build lifts a fragile, broken body off the dirt, providing a clean space to heal. Along with each bed, we deliver essential survival packs of food, clean water, and thermal warmth.
