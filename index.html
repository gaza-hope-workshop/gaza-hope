From Rubble We Forge Life

We pull buried rebar from destroyed homes to hand-weld emergency beds for amputated children forced to sleep on open dirt. Your support is their only shield.

Save a Child via GoFundMe Crypto Direct Aid

Inside freezing, damp tents across Gaza, newly amputated children lie helplessly on bare dirt. Their open surgical wounds face constant threat of severe, fatal infection. We refuse to stand by and watch them bleed on the earth. Born from the ashes, Gaza Hope Workshop digs through shattered concrete with bare hands to extract buried steel rebar. We hammer, bend, and hand-weld each bar into sturdy, elevated medical beds. Every single bed we build lifts a fragile, broken body off the dirt, providing a clean space to heal. Along with each bed, we deliver essential survival packs of food, clean water, and thermal warmth. An amputated child is crying out in pain right now — help us weld their bed tonight and turn despair into victory.

Defying Destruction

We turn instruments of war into weapons of mercy. Every bed we forge is pulled from the very rubble that shattered these children's lives. We don't offer sympathy — we deliver real, tangible protection, warmth, and life-saving dignity.
