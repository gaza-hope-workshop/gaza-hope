Wounded survivors are sleeping on the ground tonight

They survived the blast.
Now they sleep on bare, freezing ground — wounds open.

Inside freezing, damp tents in Gaza, newly amputated children and young survivors lie on bare dirt, their surgical wounds exposed to constant risk of infection. Gaza Hope Workshop pulls twisted iron bars from the rubble and hand-welds them into raised emergency beds — lifting fragile bodies off the ground so they can finally heal.

A newly amputated survivor is waiting in a cold tent right now.
